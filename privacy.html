Privacy Policy

CatRec – Screen Recorder · Last updated: October 11, 2026

This policy explains how CatRec – Screen Recorder (the "App"), published by ibbie ("we", "us"), handles information. We don't run servers, the App has no accounts, and we don't sell your data.

In short: your recordings, screenshots and audio are saved on your phone and never sent to us. The free version shows Google ads. The App sends anonymous crash reports and usage statistics to Google Firebase, which you can turn off in Settings.

Your recordings stay on your phone

The App captures your screen only after you start a recording or screenshot and approve Android's screen-capture prompt. Android shows that capture is active for as long as it runs. Microphone audio, internal app audio and the face-cam picture are captured only when you turn those options on.

Everything the App creates is saved on your device:

  • videos in Movies/CatRec (or a folder you choose),
  • screenshots in Pictures/CatRec/Screenshots and GIFs in Pictures/CatRec/GIFs,
  • separate microphone tracks, if you turn them on, in Music/CatRec or Recordings/CatRec.

The App never uploads these files. They leave your phone only if you share them yourself. Because they are saved in your phone's shared storage, they stay there if you uninstall the App; delete them from the App or your gallery.

Settings and app data on your device

The App keeps your settings (for example resolution, frame rate, audio sources, overlay options, theme and language), whether you bought Remove Ads, when a temporary Pro unlock expires, and a random install ID used only for crash reports and statistics. This stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

Game mic helper (optional accessibility service)

CatRec includes an optional Android accessibility service called CatRec game mic helper. CatRec is not an accessibility tool, and you don't need this service to record. On supported devices it lets CatRec keep recording your microphone while a game or chat app is also using it, so your teammates can still hear you.

You turn it on yourself in Android's Accessibility settings, after reading an in-app explanation and choosing to continue, and you can turn it off there at any time. The service:

  • cannot read what is on your screen or inspect other apps (canRetrieveWindowContent is off),
  • cannot tap, type, scroll or control your phone (canPerformGestures is off),
  • receives only window-change notices from Android, which can include the name of the app in front, and ignores them. Nothing from them is stored or sent anywhere,
  • does not record audio. Your microphone is recorded by CatRec's recorder, with the Microphone permission, only during a recording you started with the microphone on.

No personal or sensitive data is collected or shared through the accessibility service.

Advertising

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob: banner ads, an ad when you open the App, and reward videos that you choose to watch for a temporary Pro unlock. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • device and app information (model, Android version, language, app version),
  • approximate location from your IP address,
  • ad interactions (which ads were shown or tapped).

Google uses this data as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can turn off Personalized ads in the App's Settings, after which the App asks only for non-personalized ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Buying Remove Ads stops all ads.

Crash reports and usage statistics

The App uses Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics (Google) to find crashes and see which features are used. They receive:

  • crash reports and diagnostic logs, including recording settings such as resolution and encoder,
  • app version, device model and Android version,
  • events such as "recording started" or "screenshot taken",
  • the random install ID described above.

They never receive your recordings, screenshots, audio or anything shown on your screen. This is on by default; turn off Usage analytics in Settings to stop both and clear the install ID. Google keeps Crashlytics crash data for 90 days. See the Google Privacy Policy and Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Purchases

Remove Ads and the optional Support me tip are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details; Google tells the App only whether a purchase went through so it can unlock what you bought.

Ratings

The App may occasionally show Google Play's built-in rating prompt. Google handles it, and we don't learn whether you rated the App or what you wrote.

Emailing us

If you contact us from the App, your email app opens with a message you can edit before sending. The App adds the app version, your device model and your Android version to help us fix problems, and you can add screenshots or recordings. We receive only what you send, use it only to answer you, and delete it when it is no longer needed.

Who we share data with

Only the Google services named above (AdMob, Firebase and Google Play), which process data for us under their own policies. We don't sell your data or share it with anyone else.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has sent us personal information, email us and we'll delete it.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn off Usage analytics or Personalized ads in the App's Settings.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Turn off the game mic helper in Android's Accessibility settings, or remove any permission in the App's system settings.
  • Delete your recordings at any time, and uninstall the App to delete its settings.

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you, or to object to how it is used. We don't hold your name or contact details unless you email us, and data held by Google services isn't linked to your identity, so we usually can't single it out. Email us and we'll help as far as we can. You can also contact Google about data its services hold.
