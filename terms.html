1. What the App does

CatRec records your Android screen, with internal audio and your microphone where your device allows it. It also keeps the last few minutes in a rolling buffer (Clipper), takes screenshots, and adds floating controls, on-screen drawing, a face cam and a watermark. It can trim, merge and convert your clips to GIFs. Features depend on your device, Android version and manufacturer; some apps block screen or audio capture, and the App can't get around that.

The optional CatRec game mic helper uses Android's accessibility service only to keep your microphone recording while a game or chat app also uses it. It is not needed for ordinary recording and you can turn it off at any time. See the Privacy Policy and Permissions page for exactly what it can and can't do.
