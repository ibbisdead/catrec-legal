CatRec – Screen Recorder

A screen recorder for Android, made for gamers.

Record your screen with internal audio and your microphone, keep the last few minutes with Clipper, take screenshots, add a face cam and draw on screen, then trim, merge or turn clips into GIFs. Your recordings stay on your phone.
