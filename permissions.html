Permissions

CatRec – Screen Recorder · Last updated: October 25, 2026

This page lists every Android permission CatRec uses, what it is for, and whether you can say no. None of them is used to send your recordings, screenshots or audio to us. How data is handled is covered in the Privacy Policy.

Screen capture

Recording the screen doesn't need a permission listed in the app; instead, Android asks you every time a recording or screenshot starts, and shows that capture is active until it stops. On Android 14 and later you can choose to share a single app instead of the whole screen.

Internal audio (the sound of your games and apps) uses Android's audio playback capture, which works through that same screen-capture approval on Android 10 and later. Some apps don't allow their audio to be captured, and CatRec can't override that.

About the game mic helper

CatRec is not an accessibility tool, and the helper isn't needed to record. You can open its explanation from the Game mic button (the headset icon next to the capture-mode controls), or from Fix game chat mic in Settings. Only after you tap Agree and continue does CatRec open Android's Accessibility settings, where you switch on CatRec game mic helper yourself. Choosing No thanks or leaving the screen is not treated as agreement. Turn it off any time in Settings → Accessibility (the exact path varies by phone).

  • It cannot read your screen or other apps (canRetrieveWindowContent="false").
  • It cannot tap, type, scroll or control anything (canPerformGestures="false").
  • It asks Android for no events at all, so it receives no information about your screen or the apps you use.
  • It doesn't record audio itself; CatRec's recorder does that with the Microphone permission, only during a recording you started.

Choosing files

When you pick an image or video yourself (a watermark image, clips to merge, a video to turn into a GIF, or an attachment for an email to us), CatRec uses Android's photo picker, so it only sees the items you choose. If you pick a different folder to save recordings in, Android's folder picker gives CatRec access to that folder only.
